# Agriculture & Commodity: user guide draft

Ask CeyNex for a tea export trend, a cinnamon producer-price trend or forecast,
or an explanation of its supporting figures. Each answer presents its evidence
and limitations. A question phrased in quarters may receive an annual forecast
with a frequency warning because the registered cinnamon model is annual.

Read the source and period shown with each figure. `TEA_BOARD` is the system's
name for a curated tea workbook: its 2011–2016 annual export totals cite
Central Bank tables and its 2017–2025 totals cite Tea Exporters Association
tables. A comparison between 2011 and 2025 is a full-span change, not a
five-year change. The cinnamon producer-price forecast uses FAOSTAT annual
data and should not be interpreted as a grade-specific purchasing quotation.

Treat an 80% prediction interval as an uncertainty range, not a guarantee.
The cinnamon model's held-out coverage was only 1/3 against its nominal 80%
interval, so CeyNex reduces its confidence and displays that limitation.
When two sources disagree materially, the answer may show a `dq_flag` with
both original numbers; it does not replace either source value.

If you ask for the district with the largest cinnamon export share or the
effect of a tea-price change on rubber demand, CeyNex may decline to quantify
the answer because the required sourced district shares or substitution
relationship are absent. WITS tariff figures are not available in this
prototype. Do not treat these answers as purchasing or trade advice.
